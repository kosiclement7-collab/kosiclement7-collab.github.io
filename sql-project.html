A SQL data analysis project examining sales performance, revenue, product performance, customer spending and business trends.

The objective of this project is to analyze sales transaction data and generate useful business insights that can support decision-making.

The analysis focuses on revenue, sales volume, products, categories, customers and sales trends.

SQL Analysis

Example of the SQL queries used in the analysis:

SELECT product, SUM(total_sales) AS total_revenue FROM sales GROUP BY product ORDER BY total_revenue DESC;

This query ranks products according to the revenue they generated.

Product Performance

Product-level analysis was performed using SQL aggregation functions such as SUM() and GROUP BY.

SELECT product, SUM(quantity) AS units_sold FROM sales GROUP BY product ORDER BY units_sold DESC;

The Mouse was the best-selling product by quantity, with 15 units sold.

Category Performance

Revenue was grouped by product category to determine which category contributed the most to overall sales.

SELECT category, SUM(total_sales) AS total_revenue FROM sales GROUP BY category ORDER BY total_revenue DESC;

Electronics was the strongest revenue-generating category.

Monthly Revenue Analysis

SELECT strftime('%Y-%m', order_date) AS month, SUM(total_sales) AS monthly_revenue FROM sales GROUP BY month ORDER BY month;

Monthly analysis was used to compare revenue performance across the available sales periods.

  • Electronics was the strongest revenue-generating category.

Business Recommendations

  • Maintain adequate inventory for high-volume products.
  • Focus marketing efforts on high-revenue products and categories.
  • Analyze lower-performing products to identify opportunities for improvement.
  • Use customer spending data to identify high-value customers.
  • Continue monitoring monthly revenue trends to support future sales planning.
